# W02 — Throgg 실전 운영안 (9.0.3, 조건부 실전 추천)
## 목표
얼음 트롤 조기 모집 + 노스카 부족 지도자 격파/통합으로 후방 구축 + Trollkind 순유입 유지 + Troll Empire Tier III/V1 증원 + 항구/전리품 복귀로 경제 안정, 4 hunts/Troll King's Lair/Rocky Throne 단기 목표 후 3 Troll Den/TierVI 장기 목표.

## 초반 루트(실제 순서는 동적)
- 1~5턴: 인접 부족장 위치와 세력을 확인, 승리 가능한 전투 우선. 지도자 격파 후 즉시 confed 여부 판단, 종속 군단 유지비 점검. Initial nearby Broken Wheel Black Rock, Aesling Forbidden Citadel, Sarl Encampment, Tallymen of Pestilence Forest Decay, Naglfarlings Naglfari Plain.
- 5~15턴: Ice Troll Lair (2k gold/2 turns)로 일반 및 얼음 트롤 양산. 일반 방패보병/미늘창 또는 Fimir를 소수 편입해 트롤 군단 정면 스크린, 빠른 스킨 울프/기마 척후로 후방 화력 제거. Trollkind resource producer first, net consumption positive; no early resource-consuming economic buildings excessively.
- 15~30턴: 북방 부족/울프릭 confed opportunity (defeat his lord), protect key ports/provinces, choose manageable battle fronts vs Malakai Dwarfs/Skaven/Kislev. pursue 4 hunts and landmark rather than pure map paint. Approach mid-tier Monster Horde cautiously.
- Turn counts heuristic, not reproduced speedrun.

## 경제/건축
- baseline 2 separate currencies: gold from battle loot/raiding/ports/infrastructure; Spoils from pillage-and-raze courier transfer to owned settlement; Trollkind from troll armies and Troll Empire buildings (not interchangeable).
- Resource bottleneck decision: gold shortage→income/ports/loot; Trollkind shortage→productive kingdom buildings, do not overuse maintenance consuming branches. Watch exact per-turn costs.
- Occupy major strategic ports, Troll Dens, Lair of Troll King, special caves; pillage-and-raze undefendable worthless enemy regions; escort Spoils couriers.
- Raid camp stance upkeep reduction confirmed practice community but actual tooltip check for own territory.
- All 9.0.3 fixed numbers from W01. Throgg-only unique building names/costs absent standard building table; do not invent.

## Technology skill priorities
- Natural Selection → Elder Trolls fast enough to secure leadership and MA/MD, then tech wolves Blood-Fuelled Hunger, monster buffs Violent Evolution (rank7+ ranged resistance). Global recruit when projecting power, rest economy.
- Lord level12 Lord of Beasts→Mutant Regeneration / Monstrous Fiends. Rank18 Battle-Hardened (faction heal cap), rank22 Unite Creatures (factionwide special trolls +2 horde). Skill prereqs to inspect.
- Throgg personal DPS useful but faction buff has multiplier value when heavy Troll stack.

## Army sizes and roles (20 max)
- Early mixed: Throgg1, hero1, Troll9, Skin Wolf2, infantry/fimir4, horse skirmish3.
- Mid troll-heavy: Throgg1, Fimir Balefiend1, Werekin or Fimir hero1, Troll13, Skin Wolf3, infantry/Fimir1.
- Heavy front flame missiles dwarfs/skaven → troll count lower, more fast interceptors/hero magic. Full 17-18 Troll experimental situational.
- Special troll role: Ice default price/performance; River melee defense/aura; Bile HP+MD; Armoured Chaos high armor, Stone magic resistance. Overall power not reducible to weapon strength only.

## Battle matchup
- Trolls large monster infantry (16), low base leadership (~45) + regen but vulnerable fire, ranged suppression, anti-large. Keep close own Lord leadership aura. Protect model counts, don't just solo charge a spear wall. Approach flank with cover and wolves/throwing axe fast cavalry; pin front with infantry/fimir and hit clustered infantry with Troll body models; healing cap not infinite.
- Malakai dwarfs: gyro/artillery/flamers threat; avoid open charge, target artillery, don't stack on flame lines.
- Skaven: isolate Ratling/warpfire missile; expand formation and early wolf flanks.
- Kislev/Empire: hybrid missile and gunners artillery; don't stand at range.
- Chaos Warrior heavy armor: Fimir great weapons, magic and focus fire.
- Auto-resolve damage isn't equal manual battle micro effects.

Short: four hunts, III Troll-kin March, own Lair of Troll King, Cave of Rocky Throne landmark. Reach short before distributing raids abroad if victory-oriented.
